# Cutouts

Remove backgrounds from photos, privately in your browser.
A free tool by DIMIFICAL.

Try it: https://dimifical.github.io/Projects

## How to use
1. Click "Click here to upload an image."
2. Wait for the cutout. The first time, it shows the download
   percentage of the background removal model.
3. Use the zoom buttons to inspect the result, then download it
   as a PNG. If you're on a computer, you can also use the
   scroll wheel to zoom.

For the best results, use a clear photo of one subject on a plain
background.

## Privacy
Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded to any
server. An internet connection is needed to load the background
removal library and model, which your browser may cache afterward.
