# Cutouts

Remove backgrounds from photos, privately in your browser.
A free tool by DIMIFICAL.

Try it: https://dimifical.github.io/Projects

## How to use
1. Click "Click here to upload an image."
2. Wait for the cutout. It shows download percentage.
3. Use the zoom buttons to inspect the result, then download it
   as a PNG. If your on a computer, you can use the scroll wheel as well.

For the best results, use a clear photo of one subject on a plain
background.

## Privacy
Images are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to any server.
(An internet connection is needed the first time to download the background removal model.)
